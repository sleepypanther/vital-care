Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

VitalCare ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, contact details, and health profile. We also collect data from connected health sensors (heart rate, blood pressure, SpO₂, sleep, steps) and your interactions within the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to provide the VitalCare service, generate daily wellness plans, monitor clinical thresholds set by your doctor, and communicate alerts. We do not sell your personal or health data.

3. Data Sharing

We share data only with your authorized clinicians, designated caregivers (per your consent settings), and service providers who help operate the platform under confidentiality obligations. We never share data for advertising.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption at rest and in transit, access controls, and audit logging of clinical changes. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we apply defense-in-depth.

5. Your Choices

You may revoke caregiver access, adjust consent settings, or request data deletion through the Caregiver Access page or by contacting us. Deleting data may remove functionality.

6. Children

VitalCare is not intended for use by children under 16 without parental consent and clinician oversight.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy and will post the revised version with a new effective date.
